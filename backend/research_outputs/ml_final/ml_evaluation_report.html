Why is R² low / negative across all models? Because the target metric is Customer Pickup Date minus Intake Date. An unconstrained human behavioral variable (when the customer felt like picking up their shoes) cannot be linearly or deterministically derived from shoe material, service codes, or pair quantities alone.

2. Academic Defense Diagnosis: The "Order-to-Claim" Target Constraint

A. Empirical Truth of Historical Paper Receipts

In Shoelotskey's physical manual workflow (2025–2026), paper claim slips only had two dates marked by hand:

  • Date Received: When the customer dropped off the shoes.
  • Claimed Date: When the customer physically returned to pay the balance and collect their shoes.

The physical slips never recorded when the cobbler/technician finished glue curing or sole stitching. Therefore, calculating (claimed_date - date_received) inevitably captures Customer Latency (e.g. customer waiting 2 weeks after notification before picking up shoes).

B. Why This Defends the Research Score

When the panel asks: "Why is R² low and why didn't you achieve 85% within ±2 days?"

Authoritative Student Defense Talking Point:

“Our 119 records are 100% genuine Shoelotskey empirical data. We deliberately rejected fabricating synthetic timestamps to artificially boost R² to 0.95. The low R² proves an essential real-world domain finding: customer pickup turnaround cannot be modeled solely by shoe repairs without tracking intermediate operational stages. That is precisely why our new system implements Stage-by-Stage Pair Tracking for future cohort training.”

System Dual-Timeline Architecture (Operational Security)

1. Official Customer Release Date (Operational Promise)

Determined strictly by documented, non-additive Business Rules (e.g., 25 days for Full Reglue, 10 days for Basic Cleaning). Guaranteed, auditable, and binding on customer claim slips.

2. Machine Learning Advisory Estimate (Internal Analytics)

Provided by Random Forest Regressor as advisory predictive intelligence for the workshop supervisor. Never overrides the customer's legal promise date without staff confirmation.
