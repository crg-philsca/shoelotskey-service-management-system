2. Role Profiles & Functional Responsibilities

SYSTEM ADMINISTRATOR ADMIN

Primary technical custodian, system developer, and capstone research evaluator. Has supreme authorization across the application infrastructure, telemetry, ML pipelines, and defense audits.

  • Full read/write/delete access across all database tables and operational records.
  • Machine Learning (Random Forest) model training, hyperparameters, and feature evaluation.
  • Historical data batch import, OCR parsing engine, and CSV dry-run reconciliation.
  • Research Evidence Portal (ISO/IEC 25010 Expert Surveys, TAM user evaluations, audit logs).
  • User account management: create, edit, unlock, deactivate, or delete user credentials.
  • Disaster recovery, SQLite/PostgreSQL cloud sync, and complete database rollback.
BUSINESS OWNER OWNER

Store executive and business decision-maker. Oversees daily operations, service catalogs, store financials, staff productivity, and customer resolution.

  • Store dashboard: revenue trends, active order volume, overdue claims, and status distribution.
  • Sales reports: real-time sales analytics, payment breakdowns (Cash vs. GCash), and tax summaries.
  • Expense logging: track operational expenses (supplies, utilities, equipment maintenance).
  • Service catalog: add, edit, reorder, and set prices for Base Services, Add-ons, and Rush Fees.
  • Discounts management: create and configure discount rates (Senior/PWD, percentage, fixed amount).
  • User management: manage staff accounts, reset forgotten passwords, and restrict compromised sessions.
  • Full Job Order lifecycle: create, modify, release, and print claim slips.
FRONT DESK STAFF FRONT STAFF

Customer-facing reception and cashier personnel stationed at the counter. Handles shoe intake, inspection, payments, and client pick-ups.

  • Cash Register: record payments, cash drawers, tender balance calculations, and change verification.
  • Job Order Intake: examine incoming footwear, record pre-existing defects, and suggest add-ons.
  • Claim Verification: process customer claim slips via barcode scanner or order tracking code.
  • Release Calendar: inspect delivery schedules, upcoming due dates, and pending orders.
  • Inventory checking: verify in-stock cleaning agents, laces, and sole protectors for job orders.
  • Restricted from: viewing profit analytics, system audit logs, or modifying user accounts.
SERVICE TECHNICIAN (STAFF) STAFF

Workshop craftsmen executing shoe cleaning, repainting, unyellowing, reglueing, and restoration tasks in the back-of-house workshop.

  • Workshop queue: view assigned jobs, customer specific care notes, and restoration milestones.
  • Status updates: update shoe status from "Pending" → "In Progress" → "Quality Check" → "Ready".
  • Inventory usage: record usage of raw materials (cleaners, dyes, adhesive, sole guards).
  • Claim Verification & Release assistance for completed orders.
  • Restricted from: financial reports, expenses, cash drawer, and administrative controls.

5. Defense-in-Depth Security & Session Management Specifications

OWASP Top 10 Mitigation Controls

  • A01: Broken Access Control: Enforced at both client-side React router level (`ProtectedRoute`) and server-side FastAPI dependencies (`require_role`). Missing permissions return immediate 403 Forbidden and generate an immutable audit log.
  • A02: Cryptographic Failures: Passwords hashed with salted bcrypt. JWT secrets are strictly read from environment variables; application aborts startup if secret key is missing.
  • A07: Identification & Authentication Failures: Accounts are temporarily locked out after 3 consecutive failed login attempts. Session tokens expire in 8 hours, with client-side 30-minute idle timeouts.
  • A09: Security Logging & Monitoring: High-severity events (unauthorized endpoint requests, session timeouts, account locks, and profile updates) are recorded in the audit_logs database.

Multi-Session Management Architecture

  • Independent Concurrent Sessions: Multiple front desk tablets and shop workstations can operate under authorized accounts simultaneously without clobbering each other.
  • Database Authoritative Session Store: Every login generates a UUIDv4 session ID recorded in user_sessions with IP address, user agent, creation time, and revocation flag.
  • Instant Blacklist Revocation: Logging out or locking an account immediately invalidates tokens across the distributed session cache.
  • Continuous Heartbeat Validation: Client polls /auth/verify-token every 30 seconds and upon window focus, guaranteeing instant deactivation upon administrative lockout.
