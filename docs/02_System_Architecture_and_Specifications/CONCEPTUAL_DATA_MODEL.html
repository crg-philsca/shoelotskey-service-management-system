📋 Entity-Relationship Matrix & Cardinality Rules

Primary Entity Verb (Chen Diamond) Related Entity Cardinality Business Rule Description
CUSTOMER PLACES JOB ORDER 1 : M One customer can place one or many service orders over time; each job order is associated with exactly one customer profile.
STAFF / USER INTAKES JOB ORDER 1 : M An authenticated staff member or owner creates and updates job orders; each order logs the creator/intake user.
JOB ORDER CONSISTS OF SHOE ITEM (PAIR) 1 : M A job order consists of one or more physical shoe pairs (quantity ≥ 1). Each pair is tracked with its own status, photos, and specs.
SHOE ITEM REQUIRES SERVICE CATALOG M : M Each shoe pair requires one base restoration service (e.g. Basic Cleaning) and optional add-ons (e.g. Unyellowing, White Paint).
SERVICE CONSUMES INVENTORY / SUPPLY M : M Performing restoration services consumes materials (cleaning solution, glue, paint); inventory items supply multiple services.
JOB ORDER SETTLES VIA PAYMENT RECORD 1 : M An order can be settled in a single upfront payment or split across an initial downpayment and final pickup settlement.
JOB ORDER APPLIES DISCOUNT 1 : 0..1 An order may optionally apply at most one authorized statutory discount (Senior Citizen, PWD, Student, or Promo).
OWNER (USER) AUTHORIZES DISCOUNT 1 : M Applying discounts requires privilege verification with owner password confirmation and valid Senior/PWD/Student ID record.

⚡ Business Decision Gateways (Branching Logic)

Decision Point Evaluated Condition Branching Outcome (Yes / No) System & Data Impact
Priority Gate Is order requested as Rush priority? • Yes (Rush): Expedited timeline
• No (Regular): Standard FIFO queue 		Applies +50% rush surcharge to service fees; assigns shorter target turnaround date and flags priority in dispatch.
Discount Gate Does customer present valid Senior, PWD, or Student ID? • Yes: Discount granted
• No: Standard price 		Deducts 20% privilege discount from gross service total; records authorization timestamp and patron ID number.
Settlement Gate Is customer paying 100% upfront or minimum 50% deposit? • Full: Fully Paid (₱0.00 Balance)
• Deposit: Downpayment Recorded 		If partial deposit, system computes and tracks Balance Due, requiring payment prior to customer pickup.
Claim Release Gate Is balance settled (₱0.00) AND claim slip presented? • Yes: Release shoes to customer
• No: Hold item in workshop 		Transitions order to Claimed; logs exact handover timestamp, releasing staff, and closes the order lifecycle.
