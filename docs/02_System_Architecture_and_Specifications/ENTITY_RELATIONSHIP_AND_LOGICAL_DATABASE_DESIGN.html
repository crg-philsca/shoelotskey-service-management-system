⚖️ Normalization Objective & Theoretical Justification

In relational database theory (Codd, 1970; Date, 2004), database normalization is the systematic process of organizing data attributes to minimize data redundancy, prevent modification anomalies (insertion, deletion, and update anomalies), and enforce referential integrity. The Shoelotskey Service Management System rigorously adheres to Third Normal Form (3NF) across its nine core operational tables and associative entities.

1. First Normal Form (1NF) Compliance

Rule: Each relation contains atomic (indivisible) attribute values, and there are no repeating groups or arrays within a single record.

  • Elimination of Repeating Services: In the manual paper booklet system, a customer's single shoe pair often received multiple craft services (e.g., Deep Clean + Reglue + Unyellowing) handwritten on a single line. In our 1NF model, repeating services are factored out of ITEMS and mapped into individual rows in the associative table ITEM_SERVICE_MAPPING.
  • Elimination of Repeating Conditions: Initial footwear wear defects (e.g., Scratches, Sole Separation, Leather Creasing) are decomposed into individual rows in ITEM_CONDITION_MAPPING.
  • Atomic Fields: Customer names are kept clean, contact numbers follow strict E.164 / Philippine local mobile formats (09xx / +639xx), and composite physical addresses in DELIVERIES are split into atomic components (delivery_address, barangay, city, province, zip_code).

2. Second Normal Form (2NF) Compliance

Rule: The relation is in 1NF, and every non-key attribute is fully functionally dependent on the entire Primary Key (no partial key dependencies on composite keys).

  • Resolution of Composite Primary Keys: The junction table ITEM_SERVICE_MAPPING uses a composite primary key (item_id, service_id). The attribute actual_price is functionally dependent on the entire composite key—representing the frozen price charged for that specific service on that specific footwear item at the time of intake—avoiding partial dependency.
  • Decoupling Item Properties from Service Catalog: Craft service specifications (such as catalog base_price, service_code, and standard duration_days) are strictly isolated in SERVICES, ensuring that changes to future service pricing do not retroactively alter previously completed transactions.

3. Third Normal Form (3NF) Compliance

Rule: The relation is in 2NF, and no non-key attribute is transitively dependent on the primary key (no transitive dependencies X → Y → Z).

  • Elimination of Lookup Transitive Dependencies: In un-normalized schemas, storing text values such as status names or payment method names inside ORDERS creates transitive dependencies. We decomposed all status and category dimensions into dedicated lookup relations: STATUS (status_id → status_name), PRIORITY_LEVELS (priority_id → priority_name), PAYMENT_METHODS (method_id → method_name), PAYMENT_STATUSES (p_status_id → status_name), and ROLES (role_id → role_name).
  • Customer Identity Isolation: Customer attributes (customer_name, contact_number) are isolated in CUSTOMERS. The ORDERS table references only customer_id (Foreign Key). Thus, an order depends directly on the customer identifier, eliminating transitive customer phone updates across historical orders.
  • Forensic Audit Exception (NIST SP 800-92 / ISO 27001): In AUDIT_LOGS, the fields username and role are intentionally captured as immutable point-in-time snapshots rather than dynamically joined to USERS. This intentional denormalization is an industry-standard cybersecurity compliance requirement to preserve forensic non-repudiation even if an employee account is later renamed or purged.

🎯 Defense Panelist Q&A: Database Design & Normalization

These are the exact questions anticipated from Panel Chair Asst. Prof. Aron Alojado, Assoc. Prof. Mary Ann Aballiar-Vista, and Dr. Alan Agustin regarding Figures 8 and 9:

Q1: "Why do you have both Figure 8 (ERD) and Figure 9 (Logical Database Design)? Aren't they redundant?"

Verbatim Answer Script:
"Thank you for that important methodological distinction. Figure 8 is an Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD), which models high-level business entities, semantic verbs, and cardinalities (1:M, M:M) from a business-logic perspective. Conversely, Figure 9 is the Logical Database Design (LDD), which translates those conceptual entities into concrete Third Normal Form (3NF) relational tables. Figure 9 explicitly defines physical database attributes, SQL data types (e.g., DECIMAL(10,2), VARCHAR, TIMESTAMP), primary key identifiers, nullability constraints, and foreign key cascading rules (such as ON DELETE CASCADE). Presenting both ensures total traceability from business requirements to actual PostgreSQL implementation."

Q2: "How did you resolve the Many-to-Many relationship between footwear items and services?"

Verbatim Answer Script:
"In our workshop intake workflow, a single pair of shoes can receive multiple craft services (such as Deep Cleaning and Sole Regluing), while a single service is performed across thousands of footwear items. To resolve this Many-to-Many relationship into pure 1:M relationships, we engineered the associative entity ITEM_SERVICE_MAPPING. This table utilizes a composite primary key consisting of (item_id, service_id) and includes the crucial attribute actual_price. This snapshots the price charged at the exact time of transaction, protecting financial ledger accuracy even if base catalog service prices change in the future."

Q3: "How does your database handle referential integrity when an order is cancelled or deleted?"

Verbatim Answer Script:
"We enforce strict referential cascading rules at the database engine level: Dependent line-items (ITEMS), associative service bridges (ITEM_SERVICE_MAPPING), delivery records (DELIVERIES), and payment settlements (PAYMENTS) are defined with ON DELETE CASCADE linked to their parent order_id. If an order is purged, orphaned child rows are cleanly removed. Conversely, for operational audit ledgers like INVENTORY_LOGS and AUDIT_LOGS, foreign keys employ ON DELETE SET NULL to ensure that inventory stock change histories and compliance audit trails remain permanently intact for accounting review."
