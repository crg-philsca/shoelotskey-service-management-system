4 Claim Date Precision & Prohibition of Fabricated Timestamps

The Daily Sales Monitoring Reports record calendar dates (e.g. 2026-09-11) but do not document the exact hour or minute when the customer walked in to retrieve their footwear.

  • Frontend Display Protocol: The UI inspects claim_time_precision. When set to "DATE_ONLY", the UI renders 09/11/26. Under no circumstance will the UI display 12:00 AM, opening hours, or closing hours as though they were observed.
