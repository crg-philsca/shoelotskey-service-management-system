  • Timing Protocol & Stopping Rules: Start trigger: Timing initiated the moment the researchers received the target query and began flipping paper tags or typing into the search bar. Manual stop trigger: Timing stopped when the physical paper receipt was located, retrieved, and readable. Digital stop trigger: Timing stopped when the matching record was rendered on-screen and verified.
  • Defense Text Clarification (1.8 Seconds vs. 0.41 Minutes): The manuscript's Table 17 reports 0.41 minutes (24.6 seconds), representing the end-to-end human interaction time (typing customer name, visual inspection, clicking detail modal). The presentation slide citation of 1.8 seconds corresponds to the automated backend API database retrieval latency measured during ISO/IEC 25010 response time testing (TEST-01), not human search interaction. Both figures are empirically valid within their respective operational contexts.
